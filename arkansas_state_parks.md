# Arkansas State Parks Directory

This directory lists the major state parks in Arkansas that support camping, compiled for the Campground Discovery web application.

## Parks List

### Bull Shoals-White River State Park
* **Location**: 153 Dam Overlook Ln, Lakeview, AR 72642, USA (Coords: `36.3613517, -92.5738512`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (960 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 103 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Bull Shoals-White River State Park is a premier trout fishing destination below the massive Bull Shoals Dam. The riverside campgrounds feature full hookups and beautiful Ozark scenery.

### Cane Creek State Park
* **Location**: 50 State Pk Rd, Star City, AR 71667, USA (Coords: `33.9174477, -91.7671278`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (403 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 29 RV sites, 10 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Cane Creek State Park sits where the rolling hills of the West Gulf Coastal Plain meet the flat Mississippi Delta, offering excellent flat-water kayaking, fishing, and scenic wooded trails.

### Crowley's Ridge State Park
* **Location**: 2092 AR-168 North, Paragould, AR 72450, USA (Coords: `36.043365, -90.6639634`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (447 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 26 RV sites, 18 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Crowley's Ridge State Park is set in a unique geological formation of hardwood forest and sandy soil. Built by the CCC in the 1930s, it features rustic cabins, a swimming lake, and quiet campsites.

### Daisy State Park
* **Location**: 103 E Park Rd, Kirby, AR 71950, USA (Coords: `34.2336662, -93.740456`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (866 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 103 RV sites, 25 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Daisy State Park is nestled in the Ouachita Mountains on the shores of Lake Greeson, offering scenic views, boating, and access to the Bear Creek ATV trail system.

### Devil's Den State Park
* **Location**: 11333 AR-74, West Fork, AR 72774, USA (Coords: `35.7819774, -94.25268559999999`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (1,739 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 135 RV sites, 40 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Devil's Den State Park is an Ozark valley marvel featuring hand-cut CCC stone structures, unique rock crevices, wet caves, and excellent mountain biking and backpacking trails.

### Lake Catherine State Park
* **Location**: 1200 Catherine Park Rd, Hot Springs, AR 71913, USA (Coords: `34.4287378, -92.9226486`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (3,288 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 70 RV sites, 15 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Lake Catherine State Park offers lakeside cabins and campgrounds near Hot Springs. Highlights include a waterfall trail, boat rentals on the calm lake, and guided horseback riding.

### Lake Charles State Park
* **Location**: 3705 AR-25, Powhatan, AR 72458, USA (Coords: `36.0655624, -91.1528536`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (997 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 60 RV sites, 15 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Lake Charles State Park is a peaceful retreat on a 645-acre lake in northeast Arkansas, offering lakefront camping, swimming, and some of the best crappie and bass fishing in the region.

### Lake Chicot State Park
* **Location**: 2542 State Hwy 257, Lake Village, AR 71653, USA (Coords: `33.3719416, -91.1957801`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.5 (505 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 122 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Lake Chicot State Park sits on the largest oxbow lake in North America, surrounded by pecan groves. Famous for birdwatching, fishing, and spacious full-hookup campsites.

### Lake Dardanelle State Park
* **Location**: 100 State Park Drive, Russellville, AR 72802, USA (Coords: `35.2829931, -93.20298269999999`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (1,900 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 74 RV sites, 15 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Lake Dardanelle State Park is a major tournament fishing destination on the Arkansas River. The park features a world-class visitor center, fishing piers, and lakefront camping loops.

### Lake Fort Smith State Park
* **Location**: 15458 Sheperd Springs Rd, Mountainburg, AR 72946, USA (Coords: `35.6959506, -94.1188313`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (1,316 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 30 RV sites, 15 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Lake Fort Smith State Park sits in a scenic valley in the Ozarks, serving as the western trailhead for the 165-mile Ozark Highlands Trail. Modern campground and cabins.

### Lake Ouachita State Park
* **Location**: 5451 Mountain Pine Rd, Mountain Pine, AR 71956, USA (Coords: `34.618696, -93.1772925`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (2,170 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 93 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Lake Ouachita State Park is situated on Arkansas's largest lake, known for its crystal-clear water and pine-covered islands. It offers premium lake camping, boating, and scuba diving.

### Mount Magazine State Park
* **Location**: 577 Lodge Dr, Paris, AR 72855, USA (Coords: `35.1686949, -93.6260549`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (2,693 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 18 RV sites, 10 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Mount Magazine State Park sits atop the highest peak in Arkansas (2,753 feet), offering dramatic panoramic bluff views, cool temperatures, hang gliding, and premium mountaintop camping.

### Mount Nebo State Park
* **Location**: Mt Nebo, Arkansas 72834, USA (Coords: `35.2173828, -93.24667459999999`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.9 (164 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 24 RV sites, 15 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Mount Nebo State Park sits on a flat-top mountain rising 1,350 feet above the Arkansas River Valley. The park offers stunning sunrise/sunset views, historical CCC cabins, and rim trails.

### Petit Jean State Park
* **Location**: 1285 Petit Jean Mountain Rd, Morrilton, AR 72110, USA (Coords: `35.1242439, -92.9234759`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.9 (6,927 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 125 RV sites, 35 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Petit Jean State Park is Arkansas's first state park, famous for its canyon hikes, Cedar Falls waterfall, unique rock formations (like Bear Cave), and rich CCC legacy.

### Village Creek State Park
* **Location**: 201 County Road 754, Wynne, AR 72396, USA (Coords: `35.1578246, -90.7057861`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (1,121 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 96 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Village Creek State Park is a massive park on Crowley's Ridge featuring a 27-hole championship golf course, multi-use equestrian trails, two lakes, and premium full-hookup campsites.
