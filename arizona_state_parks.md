# Arizona State Parks Directory

This directory lists the major state parks in Arizona that support camping, compiled for the Campground Discovery web application.

## Parks List

### Alamo Lake State Park
* **Location**: Alamo Rd, Wenden, AZ 85357, USA (Coords: `34.2419122, -113.573311`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (474 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 250 RV sites, 50 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Alamo Lake State Park is one of the best places to fish for bass and view the starry night skies in Arizona. Nestled in the Bill Williams River Valley, the park offers premier camping, boating, and outdoor recreation.

### Buckskin Mountain State Park
* **Location**: 5476 AZ-95, Parker, AZ 85344, USA (Coords: `34.2559683, -114.1603572`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (1,246 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 80 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Buckskin Mountain State Park commands a scenic view of the Parker Strip along the Colorado River. It offers water sports, hiking trails, and a lush green campground perfect for RVs and tents.

### Catalina State Park
* **Location**: 11570 N Oracle Rd, Tucson, AZ 85737, USA (Coords: `32.436433, -110.909647`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (1,888 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 120 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Catalina State Park sits at the base of the majestic Santa Catalina Mountains. The park is a haven for desert plants, wildlife, and nearly 150 species of birds, featuring miles of hiking and equestrian trails.

### Cattail Cove State Park
* **Location**: AZ-95, Lake Havasu City, AZ 86406, USA (Coords: `34.3515143, -114.1697365`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (1,125 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 64 RV sites, 15 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Cattail Cove State Park offers a safe harbor and beach along Lake Havasu. Campers can enjoy boating, fishing, and lounging on the sandy beach surrounded by scenic desert hills.

### Dead Horse Ranch State Park
* **Location**: 675 Dead Horse Ranch Rd, Cottonwood, AZ 86326, USA (Coords: `34.7537237, -112.0215606`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (1,232 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 150 RV sites, 40 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Located in Cottonwood in the Verde Valley, Dead Horse Ranch State Park features lagoons for fishing, miles of river access, hiking and biking trails, and spacious campground loops with electricity and water.

### Fool Hollow Lake Recreation Area
* **Location**: 1500 Fools Hollow Rd, Show Low, AZ 85901, USA (Coords: `34.2720518, -110.0695438`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (1,481 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 92 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Fool Hollow Lake offers year-round camping in the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest at 6,300 feet elevation. Camp under tall pines along the shores of a sparkling 150-acre lake.

### Homolovi State Park
* **Location**: AZ-87, Winslow, AZ 86047, USA (Coords: `35.0464771, -110.6527338`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.5 (1,170 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 53 RV sites, 10 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Homolovi State Park serves as a center of research for the migration of the Hopi people from the 1200s to late 1300s. Campers can explore archeological sites and hike through red desert grasslands.

### Kartchner Caverns State Park
* **Location**: 2980 AZ-90, Benson, AZ 85602, USA (Coords: `31.836596, -110.3488848`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (3,879 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 62 RV sites, 10 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Kartchner Caverns State Park features a massive, live limestone cave with world-class formations. The campground offers mountain views, water and electric hookups, and excellent hiking.

### Lake Havasu State Park
* **Location**: 699 London Bridge Rd, Lake Havasu City, AZ 86403, USA (Coords: `34.49233400000001, -114.3599939`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (5,060 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 47 RV sites, 10 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Lake Havasu State Park is the perfect place to enjoy the water, sandy beaches, and boat ramps on Lake Havasu. The campground features electrical hookups and modern showers close to the shore.

### Lost Dutchman State Park
* **Location**: 6109 N Apache Trail, Apache Junction, AZ 85119, USA (Coords: `33.4638509, -111.4812549`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (3,447 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 134 RV sites, 40 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Named after the legendary lost gold mine, Lost Dutchman State Park is located in the Sonoran Desert at the base of the Superstition Mountains. It offers spectacular hiking, desert scenery, and campsites.

### Lyman Lake State Park
* **Location**: 11 US-180, St Johns, AZ 85936, USA (Coords: `34.3567268, -109.3773795`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.5 (141 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 56 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Nestled in the hills of northeastern Arizona, Lyman Lake State Park is a massive 1,200-acre reservoir with no speed restrictions for boats, offering camping, cabins, petroglyph hikes, and fishing.

### Patagonia Lake State Park
* **Location**: 400 Patagonia Lake Rd, Nogales, AZ 85621, USA (Coords: `31.4934259, -110.8544923`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (2,779 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 105 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Tucked away in the rolling hills of southeastern Arizona, Patagonia Lake State Park features a 265-acre lake stocked with bass and trout, offering beach lounging, boat rentals, and water-side camping.

### Picacho Peak State Park
* **Location**: Picacho Peak, Arizona 85141, USA (Coords: `32.63506780000001, -111.40067`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (350 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 85 RV sites, 15 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Picacho Peak State Park is known for its iconic 1,500-foot peak and historical significance as the site of the westernmost Civil War clash. The campground offers beautiful desert camping with electric hookups.

### River Island State Park
* **Location**: 5200 AZ-95, Parker, AZ 85344, USA (Coords: `34.2541496, -114.1381799`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (524 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 37 RV sites, 10 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: River Island State Park is ideal for tent campers and small RVs along the Colorado River. It features a sandy beach, boat launch, hiking trails, and beautiful river canyon views.

### Roper Lake State Park
* **Location**: Roper Lake State Park, Arizona 85546, USA (Coords: `32.7561688, -109.7048509`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.5 (86 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 30 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Roper Lake State Park offers great fishing, a hot spring tub, and views of Mount Graham. The park has cabins, grassy campsites with water and electric hookups, and excellent birdwatching.
