# California State Parks Directory

This directory lists the major state parks in California that support camping, compiled for the Campground Discovery web application.

## Parks List

### Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park
* **Location**: 47895 Highway 1, Big Sur, CA 93920 (Coords: `36.2533054, -121.7832953`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (2,646 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 189 RV sites, 50 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park features redwoods, hiking trails, and beautiful campsites along the scenic Big Sur River.

### Anza-Borrego Desert State Park
* **Location**: 200 Palm Canyon Dr, Borrego Springs, CA 92004 (Coords: `33.2561225, -116.4033417`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (4,005 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 120 RV sites, 50 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: California's largest state park offers dramatic desert canyons, spring wildflowers, palm oases, and expansive camping options under dark skies.

### Morro Bay State Park
* **Location**: 60 State Park Rd, Morro Bay, CA 93442 (Coords: `35.3471229, -120.8390193`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (1,662 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 130 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Morro Bay State Park features lagoon views, sailing, hiking trails, a golf course, and a shaded campground popular with bird watchers.

### Humboldt Redwoods State Park
* **Location**: 17119 Avenue of the Giants, Weott, CA 95571 (Coords: `40.3084516, -123.9081832`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.9 (5,145 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 150 RV sites, 50 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Home to the massive Avenue of the Giants, this park offers camping deep within ancient redwood groves along the scenic Eel River.

### Crystal Cove State Park
* **Location**: 8471 N Coast Hwy, Laguna Beach, CA 92651 (Coords: `33.5809224, -117.8014755`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (7,259 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 28 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Offers scenic bluff-top camping overlooking the Pacific Ocean, hiking trails through coastal canyons, and historic beachside cottages.

### San Elijo State Beach
* **Location**: 2050 Coast Hwy 101, Cardiff, CA 92007 (Coords: `33.0212438, -117.2841606`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (1,537 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 150 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: A very popular coastal campground on the cliffs of Cardiff-by-the-Sea, offering surf-side camping and a camp store.

### Calaveras Big Trees State Park
* **Location**: 1170 CA-4, Arnold, CA 95223 (Coords: `38.2544403, -120.3532525`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (5,306 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 120 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Famous for preserving two groves of giant sequoias, the park offers wooded campgrounds along the Stanislaus River.

### Carpinteria State Beach
* **Location**: 205 Palm Ave, Carpinteria, CA 93013 (Coords: `34.3948314, -119.5178646`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (4,779 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 200 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Located right on the sandy shore, this beach park offers extensive camping, tidepools, and a relaxing coastal atmosphere.

### D.L. Bliss State Park
* **Location**: 9881 CA-89, Tahoma, CA 96142 (Coords: `39.0617236, -120.122619`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (535 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 140 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Located on the shores of Lake Tahoe, D.L. Bliss features clear blue water, sandy beaches, hiking trails, and granite cliffs.

### McArthur-Burney Falls Memorial State Park
* **Location**: 24898 CA-89, Burney, CA 96013 (Coords: `41.0167664, -121.6412627`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (2,849 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 100 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Famous for the spectacular 129-foot Burney Falls, this park offers a lush pine forest campground near Lake Britton.
