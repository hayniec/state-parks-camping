# Alaska State Parks Directory

This directory lists the major state parks in Alaska that support camping, compiled for the Campground Discovery web application.

## Parks List

### Nancy Lake State Recreation Area
* **Location**: Nancy Lake Pkwy, Willow, AK 99688, USA (Coords: `61.6620937, -150.1073699`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.4 (138 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 30 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Nancy Lake State Recreation Area is a flat, lake-filled landscape perfect for canoeing, hiking, and primitive camping. Unlike much of Alaska, it features quiet waters surrounded by birch and spruce forests.

### Byers Lake Campground
* **Location**: Trapper Creek, AK 99683, USA (Coords: `62.74690889999999, -150.117049`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.4 (68 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 73 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Located in Denali State Park, Byers Lake Campground offers spectacular mountain views, hiking trails around the lake, kayaking, and excellent fishing. No motorized boats are allowed, keeping it serene.

### Anchor River State Recreation Area
* **Location**: 74272 Anchor Point Rd, Anchor Point, AK 99556, USA (Coords: `59.77348229999999, -151.8670265`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (71 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 90 RV sites, 40 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Anchor River State Recreation Area is a premier destination for salmon and steelhead fishing on the Kenai Peninsula. Campgrounds stretch along the river down to the beach on Cook Inlet.

### Deep Creek State Recreation Area
* **Location**: 16965 Sterling Hwy, Ninilchik, AK 99639, USA (Coords: `60.027711, -151.701076`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (154 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 100 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Deep Creek State Recreation Area is famous for its tractor-launch boat service on the beach, letting saltwater anglers target massive halibut and king salmon in Cook Inlet. Scenic ocean camping.

### Ninilchik State Recreation Area
* **Location**: Ninilchik, AK 99639, USA (Coords: `60.0511677, -151.663035`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 5 (1 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 165 RV sites, 50 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Ninilchik State Recreation Area sits on a bluff overlooking the historic village of Ninilchik, Cook Inlet, and volcanic peaks. Excellent base camp for clamming and salmon fishing.

### Johnson Lake State Recreation Area
* **Location**: 21501 Crooked Creek Rd, Kasilof, AK 99610, USA (Coords: `60.29982769999999, -151.2683641`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 5 (8 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 50 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Johnson Lake State Recreation Area features a wooded campground surrounding a quiet, warm lake on the Kenai Peninsula. It offers family-friendly camping, canoeing, trout fishing, and a playground.

### Clam Gulch State Recreation Area
* **Location**: 17467 Clam Gulch Rd, Clam Gulch, AK 99568, USA (Coords: `60.238708, -151.3956181`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (89 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 120 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Clam Gulch State Recreation Area sits high on a bluff with panoramic views of Cook Inlet and the Alaska Range volcanoes. Historically famous for razor clamming on the beaches below.

### Captain Cook State Recreation Area
* **Location**: Kenai Spur Hwy, Nikiski, AK 99611, USA (Coords: `60.7829484, -151.0564326`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (129 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 40 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Captain Cook State Recreation Area offers a peaceful escape on the northern Kenai Peninsula, featuring forests, lakes, rivers, and saltwater beaches on Cook Inlet. Great for canoeing and beach combing.

### Harding Lake State Recreation Area
* **Location**: Salcha, AK 99714, USA (Coords: `64.4409776, -146.8688268`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (90 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 90 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Located southeast of Fairbanks, Harding Lake State Recreation Area is a popular interior Alaska park featuring a large lake for water skiing, swimming, trout fishing, and fully wooded campsite loops.

### Chena River State Recreation Area
* **Location**: Chena Hot Springs Rd, Fairbanks, AK 99712, USA (Coords: `64.9077365, -146.3474842`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (263 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 80 RV sites, 40 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Chena River State Recreation Area covers 250,000 acres of forested hills and river valleys near Fairbanks. Campers can paddle the river, hike the famous Angel Rocks trail, and soak in nearby hot springs.

### Quartz Lake State Recreation Area
* **Location**: Delta Junction, AK 99737, USA (Coords: `64.1981412, -145.8266396`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (82 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 100 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Quartz Lake State Recreation Area is renowned for some of the best trout and landlocked salmon fishing in Interior Alaska. The park features two campgrounds and access to scenic water activities.

### Eagle River Campground
* **Location**: 9777 Hesterberg Rd, Eagle River, AK 99577, USA (Coords: `61.3063665, -149.571764`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.4 (215 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 57 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Tucked away in the Chugach State Park near Anchorage, Eagle River Campground is a wooded campground along the rushing Eagle River, offering a perfect base for hiking alpine valleys.

### Eklutna Lake Campground
* **Location**: Mile 10, 39370 Eklutna Lake Rd, Chugiak, AK 99567, USA (Coords: `61.4091522, -149.1415779`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (281 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 50 RV sites, 25 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Eklutna Lake Campground sits along a massive, glacier-fed lake in Chugach State Park. Campers can rent kayaks, ride mountain bikes, or hike miles of trails surrounded by towering peaks.
