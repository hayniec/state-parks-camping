# Connecticut State Parks Directory

This directory lists the major state parks in Connecticut that support camping, compiled for the Campground Discovery web application.

## Parks List

### Hammonasset Beach State Park
* **Location**: 1288 Boston Post Rd, Madison, CT 06443 (Coords: `41.2731622, -72.5696867`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.5 (2,663 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 550 RV sites, 50 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Connecticut's largest shoreline park offers over two miles of sandy beach, boardwalk walking, bicycling, and a massive coastal campground.

### Rocky Neck State Park
* **Location**: 244 W Main St, East Lyme, CT 06357 (Coords: `41.317393, -72.2409786`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (4,551 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 160 RV sites, 40 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Rocky Neck features a sandy beach on Long Island Sound, salt marsh boardwalks, historic stone pavilions, and wooded campgrounds.

### Black Rock State Park
* **Location**: 2065 Thomaston Rd, Watertown, CT 06795 (Coords: `41.6298393, -73.1022639`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.5 (1,443 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 78 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Nestled in the scenic Litchfield Hills, Black Rock offers pond swimming, hiking trails to scenic vistas, and a heavily wooded campground.

### Devil's Hopyard State Park
* **Location**: 366 Hopyard Rd, East Haddam, CT 06423 (Coords: `41.4856693, -72.341798`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (1,916 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 21 RV sites, 10 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Famous for Chapman Falls and historic stone bridges, Devil's Hopyard features quiet, heavily shaded campsites along a rushing trout stream.

### Hopeville Pond State Park
* **Location**: 929 Hopeville Rd, Griswold, CT 06351 (Coords: `41.6018706, -71.9272716`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (946 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 80 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Set in a historical mill pond area, Hopeville Pond offers water sports, fishing, beach swimming, and quiet wooded campsites.

### Housatonic Meadows State Park
* **Location**: 90 US-7, Sharon, CT 06069 (Coords: `41.8378662, -73.3804096`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (251 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 95 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Situated directly along the beautiful Housatonic River, this pine-forested park is popular for fly fishing and rustic riverside camping.

### Lake Waramaug State Park
* **Location**: 30 Lake Waramaug Rd, New Preston, CT 06777 (Coords: `41.7059282, -73.3834121`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (507 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 76 RV sites, 10 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Offers spectacular water views on Lake Waramaug, canoe rentals, swimming, fishing, and scenic shoreline camping.

### Macedonia Brook State Park
* **Location**: 159 Macedonia Brook Rd, Kent, CT 06757 (Coords: `41.7697627, -73.4992124`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (222 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 51 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Macedonia Brook features a rugged trail network, mountain streams, scenic gorge views, and quiet rustic campsites.

### Mashamoquet Brook State Park
* **Location**: 147 Wolf Den Rd, Pomfret Center, CT 06259 (Coords: `41.8493562, -71.9783268`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.5 (501 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 53 RV sites, 15 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Home to the historical Wolf Den cave, the park offers wooded campgrounds, streams, hiking trails, and a pond swimming area.

### Kettletown State Park
* **Location**: 1400 Georges Hill Rd, Southbury, CT 06488 (Coords: `41.431653, -73.201621`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.5 (581 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 60 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Located on the shores of Lake Zoar on the Housatonic River, Kettletown offers extensive hiking trails, vistas, and shaded campsites.
