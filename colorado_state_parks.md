# Colorado State Parks Directory

This directory lists the major state parks in Colorado that support camping, compiled for the Campground Discovery web application.

## Parks List

### Boyd Lake State Park
* **Location**: 3720 N County Rd 11C, Loveland, CO 80538 (Coords: `40.4374091, -105.0375469`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.4 (3,023 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 148 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Boyd Lake offers water sports, sailing, fishing, a swim beach, and modern pull-through RV campsites at the foot of the Rocky Mountains.

### Chatfield State Park
* **Location**: 11500 N Roxborough Park Rd, Littleton, CO 80125 (Coords: `39.5296252, -105.0823204`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (7,165 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 197 RV sites, 40 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Nestled along the foothills near Denver, Chatfield features a massive reservoir for boating, a dog off-leash area, and full-hookup campsites.

### Cherry Creek State Park
* **Location**: 4201 S Parker Rd, Aurora, CO 80014 (Coords: `39.6201142, -104.8414341`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (9,591 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 135 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Anchored around an 880-acre reservoir, this suburban state park offers diverse trails, water sports, and a wooded campground with full hookups.

### Cheyenne Mountain State Park
* **Location**: 410 JL Ranch Heights Rd, Colorado Springs, CO 80926 (Coords: `38.7352964, -104.8194222`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (2,525 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 51 RV sites, 10 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Situated at the base of Cheyenne Mountain, this park features pristine prairie-to-peak hiking trails and modern full-hookup sites.

### Eleven Mile State Park
* **Location**: 4224 County Road 92, Lake George, CO 80827 (Coords: `38.9303324, -105.5185613`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.3 (208 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 290 RV sites, 50 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Renowned for its large reservoir, Eleven Mile is popular for trout and northern pike fishing, offering shoreline and backcountry campsites.

### Golden Gate Canyon State Park
* **Location**: 92 Golden Gate Canyon Rd, Golden, CO 80403 (Coords: `39.7736712, -105.2358694`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (2,894 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 130 RV sites, 40 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Located just 30 miles from Denver, Golden Gate features dense pine forests, rocky peaks, over 35 miles of hiking trails, and two campgrounds.

### Jackson Lake State Park
* **Location**: 26262 Highway 144, Orchard, CO 80649 (Coords: `40.3231969, -104.1167951`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.2 (1,676 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 260 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Often called the 'oasis in the plains,' Jackson Lake is a premier destination for boating, jet skiing, swimming, and stargazing.

### Lake Pueblo State Park
* **Location**: 640 Pueblo Reservoir Rd, Pueblo, CO 81005 (Coords: `38.2710678, -104.7192862`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.6 (4,465 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 400 RV sites, 50 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Boasting over 4,600 surface acres of water, this park features canyon walls, marinas, swim beaches, and extensive camping opportunities.

### Mueller State Park
* **Location**: 21045 Highway 67, Divide, CO 80814 (Coords: `38.9207139, -105.1586607`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.8 (1,851 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 132 RV sites, 20 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Located near Pikes Peak, Mueller features 5,000 acres of spring meadows, granite ridges, pine forests, and excellent wildlife viewing.

### Ridgway State Park
* **Location**: 28555 Highway 550, Ridgway, CO 81432 (Coords: `38.2229362, -107.7417861`)
* **Google Rating**: ★ 4.7 (1,890 reviews)
* **Camping Sites**: 280 RV sites, 30 Tent/Primitive sites
* **Description**: Framed by the dramatic San Juan Mountains, Ridgway features a beautiful reservoir, sandy beach, modern campsites, and paved walking paths.
